What

Translator

Chrome 138+ and Edge 148+ have a translation model built in: free, offline, and nothing leaves your machine. Other browsers do not, and a service fills the gap there. A service also translates better than the built-in model, at the cost of sending page text to it.

Kept in this browser profile and used only by the extension's background page. It is never given to a web page, and never sent anywhere except the service you chose.

Anything speaking the OpenAI chat-completions protocol works: OpenAI, Groq, Together, OpenRouter, or a local Ollama or LM Studio server at http://localhost:11434/v1, which needs no key and keeps everything on your machine.

While a service is in use, the text of the pages you translate is sent to it. That is the trade for better translation, and it is why the built-in model is preferred whenever it is available.

Languages

 

Glossary

One term per line: brand names, product names, legal terms. A phrase that is nothing but a glossary term is never sent to the model at all; inside a longer sentence the term is shielded and put back afterwards. If the model loses it, the sentence is translated again without the shield rather than left with a marker in it.

Memory

Phrases. The least recently used are dropped first once the limit is passed.

Diagnostics

Site lists, glossary and API key included. Remembered phrases are kept.
